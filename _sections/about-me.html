I graduated at IIT with a B.S. in Computer Engineering and a Minor in Game Studies and Design in the Spring of 2023. During my time there, I worked in research, which included being a research assistant at Argonne National Laboratory through the SULI program, becoming an Armour R&D Participant and working in collaboration with a project at NIST. I then continued that work through NIST's SURF program and through a contracted position following the end of that program.

Thanks to delving into the world board games I'm now pursuing my MFA in Game Design at DePaul University. Before attending DePaul, I worked on a board game called Welcome to Nowhere with a group of friends under the name HoneyCo. Since then, I've created a number of both analog and video games. I'm also interested in the interactions and communities that revolve around cooperative and multiplayer games.
