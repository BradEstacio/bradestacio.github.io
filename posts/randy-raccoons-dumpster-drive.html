Paint the town RAD!

Randy Raccoon's Dumpster Drive! was a 3D driving exploration game once believed to be a long lost tech demo by a mysterious dev team calling themselves 503 Dwellers, inspired by the likes of Kirby Air Ride's city trial mode. In the demo, you play as Randy Raccoon in the city of New Chicago, driving your dumpster-mobile around the city and collecting trash in an attempt to teach kids not to litter, while painting the town RAD.

This game was made for the S.I.C.K. - Super Indie City Konsole Jam

As project manager, I was responsible for coordinating meetings with the team, and facilitating discussion for mechanics, design, and narrative. I was also responsible for assigning tasks for programming, art, and sound team, and doing regular check-ins throughout the duration of the jam. Additionally, I assisted in programming where needed, implementing the quest and dialog system, and UI.

Gameplay Overview

In the game, you go around as Randy in your dumpster-mobile collecting trash while several cop cars constantly chasing you down. Collect enough trash and talk to the trash merchant in exchange for paint. This paint can be used to tag four locations around. The demo is considered to be complete once all four major locations are tagged, but feel free to explore the streets of New Chicago, as you'll never know what kind of trash you pick up off the streets or characters you meet along the way.
