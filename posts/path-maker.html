Map out a space, anytime, anywhere, whether fictional or physical.



This game was made as a final project alongside Path Maker as part of DePaul's Computer Gaming & Animation study abroad program to Japan in the summer of 2025.

This game was also made for the One-Page RPG Jam 2025 - Theme: Growth

Gameplay Overview

There are two way to play depending on if you want to map out a fictional or physical location. You'll need the following materials: paper, a pencil, 2d8, 1d6 and 1d4 (specifically for fictional mapping).

If you're mapping a fictional location, think of a fictional place to map out. Then, roll the dice to determine the following: the direction you're facing, the number of steps you take, what you encounter in that location, and the number of branching paths you encoutner at that point, and which path (or direction) to take.

If you're mapping out a phsyical location, the process is similar, except instead of using dice to determine direction, steps, what you encounter, branching paths and where you go next, it's all up to you to decide what you do or do not capture.

For either method, when your path making process ends is up to you.
